Pricing FAQ

Good to know

What is a credit?

One credit runs one try-on — one reference look synced onto one photo. Choosing several things to sync in a single run still counts as one credit.

Do credits expire?

No. Your credits stay on your account until you use them.

What if a try-on fails?

If a sync can't be generated, that's handled on our side. Try a clearer, front-facing photo for the best results.
