FAQ

Before you ask

Can I try makeup from a photo?

Yes. Use any photo as the look, add a clear photo of yourself, and LookSync puts that makeup on you. Sync the whole look or just lips, eyes, hair — your call.

Is my photo private?

Only you can see your photos and results. They're deleted automatically after 7 days, and you can delete them sooner anytime. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work in the browser?

Yes, on desktop and mobile — nothing to install. There's also an iOS app if you'd rather use that.
